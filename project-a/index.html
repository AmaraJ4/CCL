Product of Your Environment

Scientific Name:

Mutabilis(Latin word for "changeable")

Discovered by:

AJ Wick, 1966

Family:

A rare species of wandering creatures that are sensitive to their surroundings. They are curious by nature and tend to explore whatever environment they find themselves in.

Habitat:

The creature lives in a vast, structured environment made up of a grid. Small particles move throughout this space, creating constant opportunities for encounters. Although the grid doesn't change much, the creature gradually changes depending on what it encounters

Appearance:

The creature has a round body, three spikes on its head, and a simple face. It moves by bouncing gently and follows nearby movement. Although it may look simple at first, its appearance slowly changes depending on what and how many particles it encounters.

Key Characteristics:

  • Bounces gently as it moves.
  • Moves around by following the mouse.
  • Changes color and size.
  • Has three spikes on its head

What we know so far about Mutabilis? When Mutabilis was first discovered, it was small, with spikes on its head. At first, researchers noticed that contact with a particle changed its color. The effect seemed to be quite small, and the creature quickly continued exporing its surroundings. However, after numerous encounters, something more surprising began to happen. Its body grew larger, and the three spikes on its head become longer. What started as small, eventually became a significant change in its apppearance. Researchers believe that Mutabilis is shaped by everything it encounters. Each interation leaves a small mark, but effects accumulate over time. Although its exact limits remain unknown, the one thing that is clear is that Mutabilis is a product of its environment, and its appearance is a reflection of the world around it.
Guidelines for human interaction with this creature Mutabilis is curious and responds to movement around it. To interact with the creature, move your mouse around the screen and guide it through its environment. As it encounters more particles, watch for changes in its apperance. Be patient and observe closely. Each particle interation causes a small change in the creature's color, while its body size and three head spikes grow gradually after repeated encounters.
